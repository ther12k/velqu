   hashes file): the canonical fail-closed lane aborts on any checker
   failure. velqu fails exactly 2 fixture assertions on BOTH hosts —
   `POST /users bytes` and `C4 bytes`: the M25-005-A generated response
   encoders serialize object keys **byte-sorted**
   (`q-schema-runtime/src/encoder.rs` — "Fixed declared property order,
   byte-sorted at compile time"), while the frozen contract requires
   declaration order (`{"id":..,"name":..,"email":..}`). Checker verdicts:
   velqu 29/31 (both hosts); every baseline 27/27 (lugas, elysia2,
   raw-bun, raw-rust). The finding is reproducible and environmental
   independence is excluded (CI never runs the full checker; all measured
   C0-C3 bodies are single-key so the drift was invisible there). The
   measurements below are valid throughput/latency evidence; the
   conformance finding is owned by the framework, not the harness, and is
   reported for owner disposition. The canonical fail-closed lane re-runs
   once fixed.
