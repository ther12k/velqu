Known artifact (NOT an exporter defect): on a series only seconds old,
Prometheus rate() extrapolates the in-window increase to ~0, so the 5xx
ratio computed 0.0 immediately after the 502 despite the correct counter.

standard Prometheus semantics, and alert firing on fresh deployments is
out of scope for this validation.
